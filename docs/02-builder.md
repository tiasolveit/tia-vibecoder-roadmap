# 🟡 Track 2: Vibecoder Builder

**Goal:** Improve the product and grow real users.

You have something live. Now you find out whether anyone wants it, and you
make it good enough to keep.

Same rule as track 1: use real data, not your assumptions.

---

## 1. 🎨 تحسين الواجهة

Fix what confused people in your launch test. Clear copy, obvious buttons,
useful empty states, fewer options on screen.

**Done when:** Every screen answers "what do I do here?" within two seconds.

---

## 2. 📊 التحليلات والمقاييس

Add analytics. You need to know where users come from, what they do first, and
where they drop off.

Track three numbers only. Signup, activation, and the action you actually care
about. More than that and you will not read it.

**Done when:** You can open a dashboard and state your activation rate without
guessing.

---

## 3. 🧪 اختبار A/B

Change one thing and measure. Two versions of a page, split traffic, compare
the number that matters.

Run it long enough to be meaningful. A test that ends after two days tells you
the weather.

**Done when:** You have run one test to completion and made a decision based on
the result, not on your preference.

---

## 4. 🔄 حلقة الملاحظات

Set up one channel where users can talk to you. A form, a Discord, an email, a
button in the app. One is enough.

Then close the loop. Reply to every message and tell people when you ship what
they asked for. That reply is what turns a user into a repeat user.

**Done when:** You have a list of the top complaints, and you have answered at
least ten users personally.

---

## 5. 💵 التحقق من التسعير

Put a price on it. Free trials, free tiers, all of it hides the only answer
that matters: will someone pay?

Start with one plan. You cannot price three tiers without knowing who each is
for.

**Done when:** At least one person has paid you real money, even if it is one
person.

---

## 6. 📧 الإشعارات

Own your contact with users. An email list beats a platform audience you do not
control.

Send a welcome email, a useful update, and a re-engagement note. Nothing else
at this stage.

**Done when:** You can email your full list and roughly a third of it opens.

---

## 7. 💬 دعم العملاء

Write down every question you get asked twice. Turn each one into a clear answer
inside the product.

Support is product research. The things people ask are the things your UI does
not explain.

**Done when:** Your five most common questions are all answered inside the
product, not in your inbox.

---

## 8. 🚀 التسويق والإطلاق

Launch in public. Show the before and after, the numbers, the failures. People
respond to honest work in progress more than to launch announcements.

Pick one channel and stay there for a month instead of being everywhere for a
day.

**Done when:** One channel brought you signups you did not pay for.

---

## 9. 🗺️ خارطة الطريق

Now you know what to build next, because users told you. Write the next three
months of work and share it publicly.

Keep it short. Three items, not thirty. Ship the first one.

**Done when:** You have a public roadmap with three items and the first one is
in progress.
